# TriMed
## Overview
### Description
![Full Container](Assets/full.png)
*Image of the full, capped container*

A simple 3D-printed medicine holder to hold three different medications. Perfect to carry basic over-the-counter medications or for your daily routine! 

**Printables:** 
### Features
![Uncapped container](Assets/uncapped.png)

*Image of the uncapped container, exposing the internal divider*
* Three seperate compartments to organize medications
* A rotating internal divider, that snaps to expose one medication at a time
* 3D-printed screw-on lid.

### Why?
I designed this for my own personal use to carry over-the-counter medicine.

### Notes
* **This is not a medically approved storage container. Use at your own risk**

## Building
### Materials
* **PLA** or other rigid filament
### Printing
**Body**
* Print with **circular base on the bottom**. The cavity should be facing up.

**Lid**
* Print with **flat circular section adhered to the bed**.

**Divider**
* Print the divider with the **flat part facing down**. The skinny cylinder should face upwards into the air.

### Assembly
1. **Insert the divider into the body.** Ensure the cylinder fits into the corresponding hole in the body, as well as the bumps latch into the grooves inside the body.
2. **Screw on the lid**
